# Use Case: Travel Recommendations Graph 

## Social & Collaborative Recommendations
- Recommend me cities that my friends have visited and rated highly
- Recommend me cities that people with similar travel preferences visit
- Show me destinations where my social network has been, but I haven't visited yet
- Find places that multiple friends have recommended

## Personalization & Discovery
- Recommend me cities that I haven't visited but match my travel history
- Suggest destinations similar to places I've loved in the past
- Find hidden gems related to my favorite destinations
- Recommend places based on my preferred travel style (adventure, relaxation, culture, etc.)

## Location & Proximity Based
- Recommend me a weekend trip (i.e., a nearby destination within 3 hours)
- Find destinations accessible from my current location within a specific travel time
- Suggest a road trip route connecting multiple interesting cities
- Recommend cities near places I'm already planning to visit

## Category & Activity Based
- Find destinations in France that have attractions categorized as "for children"
- I want to visit places with history attractions but less crowded
- Recommend cities known for specific activities (hiking, beaches, nightlife, museums)
- Find destinations that offer multiple types of attractions I'm interested in

## Temporal & Event Based
- Recommend destinations with good weather during my travel dates
- Find cities hosting festivals or events during my planned visit
- Suggest seasonal destinations (e.g., ski resorts in winter, beach destinations in summer)
- Recommend places that are best visited during specific months

## Budget & Practical
- Find destinations that match my budget range
- Recommend cities with good value for money based on similar travelers' experiences
- Suggest destinations with affordable accommodation and dining options
- Find places where I can maximize my travel budget

## Advanced Graph Queries
- Show me the shortest path to visit multiple cities I'm interested in
- Find destinations that connect two places I want to visit (stopover recommendations)
- Recommend cities that serve as hubs to explore multiple nearby attractions
- Find destinations that are part of a thematic route (e.g., wine regions, historical sites)
